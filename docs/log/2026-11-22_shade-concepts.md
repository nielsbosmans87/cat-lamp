# Five shade concepts

**Role(s):** industrial design, 3D design

- Installed the shared agent kit in `.agents/` (Route B of `.agents/INSTALL.md`).
- Drew five concepts with `tools/sketch.py`: sitting cat, cat-head lantern, low-poly cat,
  gothic lantern, two cats back to back. Each sheet has front, side and top views with outer dimensions.
- The script checks every outline: steepest overhang 41° (limit 45°), all fit in Ø160 mm.
- Inputs from the owner: ring 67 mm above the lower edge (1/3 of the height), E14 bulb 60-70 mm long.

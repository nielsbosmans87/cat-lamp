# First 3D model of 1C

**Role(s):** 3D design, mechanical engineering

- Owner chose concept 1C (happy cat with heart).
- Built `cad/cat_shade.py`: two parts, body and lift-off head. Ribs 2.5 mm and 0.9 mm proud outside,
  glowing panels 1.0 mm, 12 open panels with pointed tops, heart with a window.
- Checks on the exported files: both valid closed solids, max Ø155 (limit 160), no supports needed.
  Five tiny overhang spots (0.4 to 2 mm²) and one 1.6 mm bridge under the ridge vent remain.
- Renders in `cad/renders/`. Decision on the ring: `docs/decisions/2026-11-22_ring-on-bowl.md`.
- Open points: ears look like small cones; more open panels high up; rib and panel thickness to confirm with a test print.

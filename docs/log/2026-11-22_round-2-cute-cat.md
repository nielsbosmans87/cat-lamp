# Round 2: cuter sitting cat

**Role(s):** industrial design, 3D design

- Owner chose concept 1 (sitting cat) with the lift-off head of concept 3, asked for a cuter cat and 50 mm more height.
- New size: 250 mm high, still in a Ø160 mm cylinder (widest Ø152). Ring stays 67 mm above the lower edge.
- Head lifts off at 140 mm (neck). Neck opening about Ø80 for the hand.
- Five variations (`sketches/round-2/`): big-eyed kitten, head tilt, happy cat with heart, chubby with round ears, collar and bell.
- Checks: steepest overhang 45° (the 6° head tilt is at the limit), all others 43° or less.
